# Rotunda engineer handoff checklist

Revision 0.2 · pre-feasibility commissioning aid · not for construction

Use this checklist to scope a multidisciplinary feasibility commission. A checked item should identify a named author, calculation or drawing, review status and acceptance criterion. The current website and model provide hypotheses and demand scenarios—not professional certification.

## 1. Project definition and site

- [ ] Record owner requirements, population and service targets, rotation purpose, uptime, design life and acceptable stopped states.
- [ ] Compare the full rotating city with a rotating district and a static circular-city control.
- [ ] Select candidate sites and obtain topographic, geotechnical, seismic, flood, wind, climate, water-resource and environmental baselines.
- [ ] Define regional connections for power, treated water, wastewater treatment, transport, emergency services and construction logistics.

## 2. Architecture, access and transport

- [ ] Develop buildable module plans; keep primary buildings within articulated module boundaries.
- [ ] Demonstrate daylight, ventilation, servicing, privacy, accessibility and public-realm quality in the 300 m planning bands.
- [ ] Model actual journeys, waiting, fixed-to-moving transfers, crowding and operations during partial or total stops.
- [ ] Establish protected egress, firefighter access and accessible crossing at any ring angle and in jammed conditions.

## 3. Loads, guideway and foundations

- [ ] Produce an auditable mass model by module, load case and operating state.
- [ ] Analyze gravity, traction, braking, lateral guide, uplift, thermal, fatigue, wind and seismic load paths.
- [ ] Compare wheel/rail, roller and other bearing concepts; size bogies, rails, guides, foundations and tolerances.
- [ ] Analyze differential settlement, module articulation, derailment containment and local support failure.
- [ ] Demonstrate inspection, jacking and replacement of one support zone without unsafe progressive effects.

## 4. Drive, braking and controls

- [ ] Size distributed drives and independent service/emergency brakes from verified inertia and resistance models.
- [ ] Set controlled and emergency stopping envelopes, jerk limits, wind thresholds and restart criteria.
- [ ] Complete machinery risk assessment, functional-safety allocation, fail-safe states and independent overspeed protection.
- [ ] Define black-start, power-loss, communication-loss, fire, seismic and manual rescue sequences.

## 5. Potable water

- [ ] Confirm source capacity, treatment responsibility, fixed loop routing and ring demand/peak factors.
- [ ] Model transfer-station duty, ring equalization storage, pressure zones, water age and disinfectant residual.
- [ ] Validate sanitary dry-break coupling or alternate interface for leakage, contamination, wear and missed docking.
- [ ] Provide N+1 pumping/transfer capacity, double isolation, backflow protection, flushing, sampling and drain containment.
- [ ] Develop a separate fire-water strategy; do not count the domestic-water allowance as fire reserve.

## 6. Wastewater

- [ ] Model gravity branches, sector sumps, pressure sewers, wet-weather inflow, surge, storage and treatment capacity.
- [ ] Validate a solids-rated no-drip transfer interface for blockage, ragging, grit, gas, odor, corrosion and cleaning.
- [ ] Demonstrate isolation and containment after a missed dock or leak, and define safe bypass/haulage for extended outage.
- [ ] Keep potable-water and wastewater equipment physically separate with independent drainage and maintenance access.

## 7. Power and other utilities

- [ ] Produce whole-city and ring load profiles including drives, pumps, transit, life safety and central plants.
- [ ] Compare conductor rail, cable management, inductive transfer and stationary-energy alternatives by loss, fire risk and maintainability.
- [ ] Define redundant feeds, sectionalization, energy storage, grounding/bonding and stopped-ring service continuity.
- [ ] Coordinate communications, district cooling/heating, refuse and hazardous-material restrictions.

## 8. Demonstrator and evidence gates

- [ ] Build a representative full-size guideway, articulation, drive and brake segment.
- [ ] Test representative structural load, settlement, vibration, stopping, support replacement and fault recovery.
- [ ] Test potable and wastewater transfer rigs separately through endurance, contamination, leakage, blockage and power-loss cases.
- [ ] Set quantitative pass/fail criteria before testing and retain independently witnessed results.
- [ ] Update the hazard log, cost plan and concept only after each evidence gate is reviewed.

## 9. Delivery, cost and approvals

- [ ] Prepare coordinated concept CAD/BIM, interface control documents and discipline responsibility matrix.
- [ ] Develop capital, operating, replacement, downtime, contingency and decommissioning estimates with stated accuracy.
- [ ] Identify applicable building, transport, machinery, water-quality, environmental and occupational-safety approvals for the host jurisdiction.
- [ ] Establish procurement, commissioning, operator training, inspection and asset-management strategies.

## Release statement

Revision 0.2 is suitable for communicating the proposal and commissioning feasibility work. It is not a validated schematic design, permit set, tender package or construction document. Those milestones require site-specific professional work and demonstrated full-scale interfaces.
